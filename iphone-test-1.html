NO SCRIPT RAN — this box was never rewritten

Pinch test

Pinch outwards on the text below, then photograph the blue box. The side bars turn green if the page managed to re-wrap the text.

The question this page exists to answer is a narrow one. When a reader pinches to magnify a page, the browser enlarges what was already laid out, so the lines become wider than the screen and each one runs off both edges. The reader sees bigger letters and half a sentence at a time, which is not reading.

A page can respond to that, if the browser tells it what the reader can actually see. Where it does, the column can be narrowed to match, the text re-wraps, and the reader gets bigger letters and whole lines together. Where the browser says nothing, the page must simply behave as it always has.

So there are only two acceptable outcomes here, and this page distinguishes them. Either the numbers above move as you pinch and this paragraph re-wraps to fit your screen, or nothing at all happens and the page magnifies as usual. What must never happen is something in between.
